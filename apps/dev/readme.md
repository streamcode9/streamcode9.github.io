This simulator works in real-time by default, matching your system's current hour to the real-life patterns of a software developer. To make sure you don't have to wait 24 hours to see everything, it also includes a Time Accelerator / Fast-Forward mode so you can watch a full 24-hour cycle of coding, sleeping, eating, commuting, and bug-fixing in just a couple of minutes!

## The app features:
* Dynamic SVG visualizer: An animated, reactive developer workspace that visually morphs depending on the state (day/night cycle, desk glow, coffee cup steam, sleeping animations, gym workouts).
* Real-time & Accelerated Modes: Instantly switch between true-to-life tracking and high-speed simulation.
* Vibrant Dashboard & Statistics: Track Energy, Satiety (Hunger), Happiness, Caffeine, Lines of Code written, and Bugs generated/solved.
* Stream-of-Consciousness Console: A scrolling, live-updating terminal tracking developer thoughts, Slack alerts, and compiler errors.
* Random Life Events: Encounter unexpected production issues, StackOverflow outages, and coffee spills!

## What's Created:
### Self-Contained Real-time & Accelerated Gameplay Simulator:
* Real-time Mode: Syncs natively with your system time to show you what a developer is doing right now (e.g. sleeping at midnight, coding during focus hours, relaxing in front of the TV in the evening).
* Fast-Forward Mode: Allows you to scale up time dramatically. Watch a full day's cycle (sleeping, getting coffee, debugging production firestorms, lunch with memes, the gym, side-projects) transpire in less than 2 minutes.
### Aesthetic Visual Workspace Engine:
* Houses complex, dynamic vector graphics (SVG) styled inside the single source file.
* Includes custom visual transitions, glowing displays, floating bedroom "Zzz" animations, running exercises at the gym, and steaming ramen bowls.
### Advanced Stat Mechanics & Interactive Dev Logger:
* Tracks Energy, Satiety, Happiness, and Caffeine in real time, fluctuating depending on what the developer is actively engaged in.
* Generates dynamic stats like Lines of Code Written, Bugs Introduced, and Bugs Resolved.
* Emits stream-of-consciousness thought bubbles and spontaneous events (e.g., Slack messages from bosses, StackOverflow downtime, PR approvals, or tragic coffee spills).
